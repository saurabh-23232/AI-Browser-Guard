How it works

A guard that sits between your people and the model

Every decision is made locally by a deterministic policy engine. No AI guessing, no cloud round-trip.

  1. 1

    Detect

    Pastes, uploads, prompts and downloads are scanned in the browser for credentials, personal data, internal IPs and your own keywords.

  2. 2

    Decide

    Your policy picks one outcome: Allow, Warn, Require authorization or Block.

  3. 3

    Authorize

    When a manager approves, a company password allows that one action for 60 seconds. It can never override a hard block.

  4. 4

    Audit

    Every decision is written to a hash-chained log. Verify it, export it to CSV or JSON, and review incidents. Prompts and files are never stored.

    Honest about what it is

    AI Browser Guard is browser-level protection, not an operating-system DLP. We would rather tell you the limits than overpromise.

    • Early access: built and tested against a local simulation of an AI site. Run a pilot on your own AI sites before wide roll-out.
    • Downloads are controlled by file type, size and source; their contents cannot be inspected before saving.
    • Images and encrypted files cannot be read, so they follow your “unsupported files” rule and are never treated as safe.
    • The audit log is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof against someone with full control of the computer.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Does it send our prompts or files to you?

    No. Scanning and decisions happen locally in the browser. The extension has no cloud service, no telemetry and does not call any AI API. Only a content-free summary (such as “1 credential found”) is stored in the local audit log.

    Which AI sites are supported?

    ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity are built in. Admins can add any other site or block one entirely. Sites you add need a one-time browser permission granted by the admin.

    Can an employee bypass it with the password?

    Only for actions your policy marks “require authorization”, and only once for 60 seconds. Hard blocks, such as passwords, API keys and private keys, cannot be overridden by any password.

    What happens when the free trial ends?

    Protected actions are blocked until a license is activated, and existing settings and logs are kept. The admin sees a Buy a license button that opens an email to us with the installation ID filled in. Licenses are verified offline.

    Is it a replacement for endpoint or network security?

    No. It is a focused layer that closes the AI-prompt gap in the browser. Keep your existing endpoint, MFA and network controls.

    Is my data safe if the admin forgets the password?

    Passwords are stored only as hashes and cannot be recovered. Keep them in your password manager and export the audit log regularly.
